Our mission & expertise

Decades of security experience, in one team.

Cozaint® is built on a team of serial entrepreneurs, technical developers, and security and law enforcement professionals with decades of security expertise.

Cozaint has developed a patent-pending video surveillance storage solution that dramatically lowers the cost of recording, storing, and managing surveillance video. Cozaint also manufactures artificial-intelligence based ‘smart’ physical security kiosks that provide 24/7/365 surveillance. With an internally developed software platform, Cozaint’s solutions augment the effectiveness of human security guards with advanced SMART technologies.

Established in 2018, Cozaint Corporation, founded by experienced physical security industry professionals, is bringing stationary security robots to organizations of all sizes.
