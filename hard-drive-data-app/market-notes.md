# Surveillance storage market notes

Newest month at the top. Written by the monthly price agent.

## 2026-10-16 (first entry)

**Prices.** Hard drive prices rose about 50% year over year by mid-2026 as AI
data centers bought up supply; Western Digital says it is sold out for 2026 and
Seagate's capacity is fully allocated. LTO cartridge prices stayed roughly flat
over the same period. Relief on drive prices is not expected before 2027.
Sources: [HDDHunt](https://hddhunt.com/blog/why-hdd-prices-are-rising-2026/),
[DatacenterDisk](https://datacenterdisk.com/price-forecast),
[TapeBackup.org](https://tapebackup.org/lto-tape-price-trend)

**Market size.** Surveillance storage is estimated at about $10.9 billion in
2026, growing about 7% a year to $15.5 billion by 2031. NAS holds the largest
share; SSDs are the fastest-growing media.
Source: [MarketsandMarkets](https://marketsandmarkets.com/PressReleases/video-surveillance-storage.asp)

**Competitors and alternatives.**
- Disk recording appliances: Milestone Husky, Genetec Streamvault, Quantum
  VS-HCI (formerly Pivot3; up to about 6 PB raw per group; no public prices).
  [Quantum](https://quantum.com/vs-series)
- Cloud: Wasabi Surveillance Cloud offloads older video from the local
  recorder to Wasabi's cloud; no egress fees.
  [Wasabi case study](https://wasabi.com/blog/case-study/clearview)
- Tape: XenData LTO archives (mainly broadcast); IBM and the LTO Program
  promote tape mostly as a backup copy behind disk.
  [SecurityInfoWatch](https://securityinfowatch.com/video-surveillance/article/12189607/the-resurgence-of-tapebased-video-storage)
- Fujifilm's article on tape for surveillance names Cozaint askALICE as its
  example of a disk-plus-tape system that plays back from either tier.
  [Fujifilm](https://datastorage-na.fujifilm.com/?p=5063)
