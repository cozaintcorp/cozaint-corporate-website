Compact, standalone security

Security at a fraction of the cost.

The BOBBY-M is a small, standalone, wall-mounted surveillance and monitoring kiosk. It delivers an overarching umbrella of awareness for your organization, and it is a perfect entry point for the places below.

9.25″ × 14.8″ × 3.5″a small kiosk that fits where a tower would not
180°field of view from two 1080p high-definition cameras
Smartartificial intelligence technologies built in
Two-waycommunications, with facial recognition and access control
IoTsensors for smoke, motion and other anomalies
Always onmonitoring that never sleeps or rests
  • Connects over standard wired network, wireless, or 4G/5G cellular
  • Concierge Service for live human assistance
  • An agent dashboard with shift logs, recent activity, important contacts and reports
