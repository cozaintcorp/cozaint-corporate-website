The problem

Cameras got better. The storage budget did not.

Hard drives are expensive to keep filling. So most systems quietly cut corners: fewer frames, lower resolution and a short retention window. Then a claim, an audit or an investigation arrives after the video is gone.

6

frames per second

A common recording rate in surveillance today, to save space. What happens between the frames is never stored.

30

days of retention

A typical retention window. After that, older video is overwritten to make room for new recordings.

2

years to file a claim

In California, a slip-and-fall claim can be filed for two years. The video that could answer it is usually long gone.

The innovation

MARCIA™ lets your video system understand LTO tape.

MARCIA is Cozaint's intelligent video lifecycle management technology. Tape is a proven low-cost way to keep huge volumes of video, but it was sold to surveillance as backup, which needs IT, a restore job and free disk. MARCIA sits between your video management system and an LTO tape library, so older video lives on low-cost tape and still comes back through the timeline operators already use.

  1. 01

    The operator works as always

    Recent days stay on disk for instant playback. For anything older, the operator simply moves the timeline in the video management system.

  2. 02

    MARCIA finds it on tape

    No backup job, no IT ticket and no free disk to restore onto. MARCIA locates the video and brings it back.

  3. 03

    The video is there in minutes

    A short wait for the video you need, in return for keeping far more of it, for far longer, at far lower cost.
