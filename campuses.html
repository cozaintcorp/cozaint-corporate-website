The challenge

College and university campuses operate like small cities. Academic buildings, residence halls, athletic facilities, laboratories, parking structures, transportation systems, student centers, outdoor spaces, and remote facilities can create sprawling surveillance environments containing hundreds or thousands of cameras. Industry research shows that campus security organizations rely heavily on video and that many are actively expanding, upgrading, or replacing their existing systems.

How MARCIA™ helps

Higher-resolution cameras can significantly improve investigative value, but they also accelerate storage consumption. MARCIA™ enables campuses to separate the economics of day-to-day video access from long-term preservation. Active footage can remain on high-performance disk while older recordings move automatically to economical LTO storage, allowing institutions to retain more historical video without continuously expanding expensive disk infrastructure.

Better video, better outcomes

That longer, higher-quality visual history can improve investigations involving theft, assault, vandalism, vehicle incidents, access-control events, residence-hall incidents, demonstrations, emergency response, and property damage. It can also provide valuable historical material for future video analytics, operational review, and AI-assisted investigations. Institutions can define retention policies appropriate to their legal, privacy, and operational requirements while gaining greater flexibility in how much video they can afford to preserve.

No new workflow for operators

MARCIA extends the existing VMS rather than introducing another operator workflow. Campus police and security personnel continue using the familiar VMS timeline to locate recorded video. MARCIA provides the underlying Intelligent Video Lifecycle Management needed to preserve Enterprise Visual Continuity across a large, distributed campus environment.
